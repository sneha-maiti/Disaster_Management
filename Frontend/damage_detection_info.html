Multimodal Neural Vision Processing Pipeline

Architecture flow from Optical Ingestion to Central Actionable Dispatch

STAGE 1: INGESTION

Optical drone survey or satellite pass received via secure API buffer.

STAGE 2: GEMINI VISION

Multimodal LLM performs semantic feature extraction and damage classification.

STAGE 3: BOUNDING BOX

Calculates damage centroid coordinates and projects visual bounding reticles.

STAGE 4: TACTICAL DISPATCH

Outputs structural integrity % and issues automated evacuation and shoring directives.
